Terms and privacy

Last updated November 2026

What Reel is

Reel is a free app for keeping track of movies and shows, rating and reviewing them, and watching together with friends. Reel doesn’t host, store or provide any videos. It plays from services you bring and sign in to yourself, such as a debrid service. You’re responsible for how you use those services and for following the laws where you live.

Using Reel

  • Reel is provided as is, without guarantees. Features can change or stop working.
  • Don’t use Reel to harass people, and don’t put anything in your name, bio or reviews that isn’t yours to share.
  • We may remove accounts that are used to cause harm.

What we store

When you have a Reel account, we keep:

  • Your profile: username, display name, picture, bio and favorites. If you sign in with Discord, your Discord name and picture are used to start it.
  • Your library: ratings, likes, reviews, diary, watchlist, what you’ve watched and where you stopped, so it’s the same on every device.
  • Friends you’ve added and requests you’ve sent.
  • Your debrid key, so every device you sign in on can play. Only you can read it.
  • Your devices: a name like “Windows” or “Fire TV” and when each was last used, so you can sign them out.

We don’t sell your information or use it for ads.

What other people can see

  • Your profile link (reelwatch.stream/@yourname) shows your name, picture, bio, favorites, ratings, reviews and watchlist to anyone who has the link. Profiles can’t be searched or listed.
  • Turn off Friends can see my ratings, reviews and watchlist in Settings to hide those from your profile and from friends.
  • Nobody can see what you’re watching right now.
  • If you turn on Show what I’m watching on my Discord profile, Discord shows it as your status.

Visit counts

The website counts how many times each page is opened per day (for example “the download page, 40 times today”). No cookies, no IP addresses, nothing about who you are.

Services Reel uses

  • Supabase hosts accounts and synced data.
  • TMDB provides movie and show information and images. Reel uses the TMDB API but isn’t endorsed or certified by TMDB.
  • GitHub hosts this website and the app downloads.
  • Your debrid service streams the video. Its own terms and privacy policy apply.

Deleting your account

In Reel, open Settings, then Account. You can download a copy of your data, or delete your account. Deleting removes your profile, library, ratings, reviews, friends and saved debrid key for good; it can’t be undone.
