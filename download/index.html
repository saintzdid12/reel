Windows

Download for Windows

If Windows says “Windows protected your PC”, choose More info, then Run anyway. Reel updates itself after that.

Mac

macOS 11 or newer
Apple SiliconIntel

Apple Silicon is any Mac from late 2020 on. Open the download and drag Reel into Applications. The first time, macOS may say it can’t check the app: open System Settings, then Privacy & Security, and choose Open Anyway.

Fire TV and Android TV

Installed with the free Downloader app
  1. On the TV, install Downloader from the Amazon Appstore (or Google Play).
  2. Open it and enter this address:
    https://github.com/saintzdid12/reel/releases/latest/download/Reel-TV.apk
  3. If the TV asks, allow Downloader to install apps, then choose Install.
  4. Open Reel and sign in with a code from Reel on your computer or phone, or with your username.

To update, enter the same address again. Your account and your place in every show stay as they are.
