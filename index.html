Reel

Track, rate and watch movies and shows — with recommendations that learn your taste, a clean macOS-style app, and friends.

Download for Windows
Windows 10 and 11
  • One-step setupConnect Real-Debrid or your own Torrentio link. Movie info is built in, and Reel updates itself.
  • Your library everywhereSign in with Discord or a Reel username and your ratings, diary and lists sync across every PC.
  • Watch togetherStart a watch party, share the code, and everyone stays in sync — with chat and reactions.
  • FriendsSee what friends are watching and rating, their favorites and reviews, and how well your tastes match.
Windows may say “Windows protected your PC” the first time — click More info → Run anyway. Reel updates itself after that.
