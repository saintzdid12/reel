Getting started

Reel is free. You need two things: a Reel account, which keeps your library, ratings and friends on every device, and a debrid service, which is where the video actually streams from.

  1. Get Reel on your computer or TV, or open it in your browser.
  2. Create an account with Discord or a username.
  3. Connect your debrid service once. Every device you sign in on uses it automatically.

Without a debrid service you can still browse, rate, write reviews and keep a watchlist. Only playing needs one.

Connecting TorBox

A debrid service is a small subscription that streams videos to you directly, fast and privately. We recommend TorBox: about $3 a month, and up to three people can stream on one account at the same time.

  1. Sign up for TorBox and pick a plan.
  2. In TorBox, open Settings and copy your API key.
  3. In Reel, open Settings. Under Connections, next to Streaming, choose Change, pick TorBox, paste the key and choose Test & save.

Reel also works with Real-Debrid, AllDebrid, Premiumize, Debrid-Link, EasyDebrid and Offcloud. The steps are the same: copy the API key from that service and paste it in Reel.

Windows

If Windows shows “Windows protected your PC” when you open the installer, choose More info, then Run anyway. That message appears for apps that aren’t from big publishers; it only happens the first time.

Reel updates itself. When a new version is ready you’ll see a note in the app; restart Reel to finish.

Mac

Pick Apple Silicon for any Mac from late 2020 on, otherwise Intel. Open the download and drag Reel into Applications.

The first time, macOS may say it can’t check the app. Open System Settings, then Privacy & Security, scroll down and choose Open Anyway.

Fire TV and Android TV

  1. On the TV, install Downloader from the Amazon Appstore or Google Play.
  2. Open it and enter the address from the download page.
  3. If the TV asks, allow Downloader to install apps, then choose Install.
  4. Open Reel and sign in with a code from Reel on your computer or phone, or with your username.

Updating

Enter the same address in Downloader again and install over the old version. Your account and your place in every show stay as they are.

Phone and browser

Open reelwatch.stream/watch and sign in. For a full-screen app, add it to your home screen: on iPhone, Share then Add to Home Screen; on Android, the ⋮ menu then Install app.

When something won’t play

  • Try another source. Open the source list on the title and pick a different one. Some files are slow or missing.
  • Check your debrid service. Make sure your subscription is active and the key in Settings is current. If you made a new key, paste it again.
  • Very new releases sometimes only have low-quality or fake files for a few days. Wait, or try a different source.
  • Picture but wrong colors on a computer: turn on Avoid Dolby Vision–only files in Settings, under Playback.
  • Out of sync audio: pick a different source; it’s usually the file.

Watch parties

On a movie or episode, choose Watch together. Send the code or link to friends; they can join from the Reel app, from a TV by entering the code, or right in their browser. Playback stays in sync for everyone, with chat and reactions. Everyone needs a Reel account and a debrid service.

Your profile and privacy

Your profile has a link, like reelwatch.stream/@yourname, that anyone can open, even without Reel. It shows your name, picture, bio, favorites, ratings, reviews and watchlist. Nobody can search for or list profiles; people need your username or link.

  • To hide your ratings, reviews and watchlist, turn off Friends can see my ratings, reviews and watchlist in Settings. Your name, picture and favorites still show.
  • When writing a review, turn on Contains spoilers and it’s blurred for everyone until they choose to read it.
  • Friends can’t see what you’re watching right now.

Your account

In Settings, under Account, you can download a copy of your data or delete your account. Deleting removes your profile, library, ratings, reviews, friends and saved debrid key for good.
